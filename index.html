Why ERK

Not the biggest recruiter. Often the last one you'll need.

We work the brief, not the spreadsheet

We don't blast requirements to a database. Every assignment begins with a scoped conversation about the team you're building and the problem you're solving.

Global reach, local ground truth

Our network spans Europe, Asia, and the Americas, but every placement is backed by local knowledge — visa realities, comp benchmarks, cultural fit.

Candidates are clients, too

We believe the market we serve is a two‑sided one. Contractors and candidates get the same level of care as hiring managers — which is why we keep both.

Niche specialisms, not generalist noise

We focus where we're genuinely useful: ERP, cloud, data, and senior engineering. If it's outside our lane, we'll say so — and point you somewhere better.

One partner across the contract lifecycle

Sourcing, onboarding, payroll, extension, offboarding. You get a single accountable contact — not a handoff chain across five internal teams.

Built for pre‑sales, too

Pitching a big programme? We provide CVs, certifications, and available resources for your pre‑sales materials, free of charge, before any engagement is signed.
